the brains behind my financial compass

20-year Navy veteran

17years · Jac&Mac Photography

Meet Jacobo Laya Jr, the founder of My Financial Compass, a 20-year Navy veteran who served as Operations Specialist and Command Financial Specialist. During his service, Jacobo honed his skills in proper money management, counseling sailors of all ranks and their families.

Jacobo's passion for financial guidance extends beyond his military career. He holds a Master of Business Administration from Excelsior College and has been a successful business owner of Jac&Mac Photography for more than 17 years.

His expertise and dedication have earned him recognition in the Marquis's Who's Who as Professional Entrepreneur of the Year.

At My Financial Compass, Jacobo's leadership and experience guide our mission to empower individuals on their journey to financial independence.

Retirement Planning

Plan for a comfortable and fulfilling retirement with our Retirement Planning service. We'll help you define your retirement goals, develop a comprehensive plan, and navigate the complexities of retirement income.

Annuities & Life Insurance with Living Benefits

Secure your financial future and protect your family's. We'll help you explore annuity options that provide a steady stream of income during retirement, and find a life insurance policy with living benefits: a safety net for your loved ones, with valuable benefits you can use during your lifetime.

Business Planning

Transform your business vision into reality with our comprehensive Business Planning service. We'll guide you through every step, from concept to execution, helping you build a successful and sustainable enterprise.

Small Business

Your business finances and your personal finances shouldn't be tangled together. We help small business owners keep them separate and well managed, so both can keep working toward your goals.

Career Planning

Getting ready to go into college? Don’t know what career field you want to specialize in? Want to find the right job for you? Debt Reduction Planning
